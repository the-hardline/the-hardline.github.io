Privacy policy

Private Mail Assistant, a private assistant for one person.

Private Mail Assistant is used by one person, its owner, with the owner's own Google account. This policy says what Google user data it accesses, how it uses, stores and shares that data, and how access is removed.

Data accessed

Through the Gmail API, with the owner's consent: the messages in the owner's own mailbox and notifications from Gmail that a new message has arrived. No other Google service is accessed, and no other person's account is connected.

How the data is used

The data is used only to help the owner manage their own mail, at the owner's direction. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, and it is not used to train general-purpose artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Sharing

Message content may be processed by service providers acting on the owner's behalf, solely to provide these features. It is shared with no one else, except where the law requires it.

Storage and retention

Any data derived from messages is kept privately and is accessible only to the owner, who can delete it at any time. The messages themselves stay in Gmail.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google account's Third-party apps and services page. Once it is withdrawn, the assistant can no longer read the mailbox.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Private Mail Assistant's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
